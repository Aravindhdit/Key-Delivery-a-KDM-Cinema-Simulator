      What’s simplified here, honestly:
      
    
        
      
      • No certificate authority chain. The theatre uses a trusted-studios list instead.
        • 
      
      • The film is encrypted with AES-128-GCM in 1 MB blocks, not AES-CBC inside MXF files.
        • 
      
      • One content key for the whole film, and a simplified XML signature instead of full XMLDSig.
        • 
      
      • This is a teaching tool, not copy protection. Once a film is decrypted in your browser’s memory, a determined person could capture it.
